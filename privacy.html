What the app accesses

With your permission, Gmail MCP uses the Gmail API (scope gmail.modify) to search, read, draft, send, label and archive email in the Google accounts you connect, only when you ask it to.

Where your data goes

The app runs entirely on your own computer. Email content is fetched directly from Google to your computer and shown to the Claude desktop app you run there. The developer operates no servers and never receives, stores or sees your email, tokens or any other data.

Storage

Sign-in tokens are stored on your computer in the operating system's credential store (Windows Credential Manager). A local list of connected addresses and a local activity log are kept in your user folder. Nothing is stored elsewhere.

Sharing

Your data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Disconnect an account in the app, or revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections. Deleting the app's folder removes all local data.
